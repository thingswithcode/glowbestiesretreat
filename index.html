FULLY GLUTEN-FREE

Gluten-free, start to finish.

Every meal at our retreats is 100% gluten-free. Not as an afterthought or a special request, but by design.

Our co-founder Leonie lives with celiac disease, so we built the whole experience around food that is safe, nourishing, and genuinely good. No cross-contamination worries, no compromises, no feeling left out.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered

Is it really 100% gluten-free?

Yes, all of it. Every meal, snack and drink at the retreat is gluten-free. Leonie, one of the two of us, has celiac disease, so this isn't a menu option we added on. It is how the whole weekend is built: no shared surfaces with gluten, no "should be fine", no exceptions.

Who is it for?

Women who run at full speed. Demanding jobs, hard training, full lives, and the need for a few days to switch off without falling behind. You don't have to be sporty or into yoga. You just have to want the break.

Where does it take place?

It moves. We pick a new location for every retreat, so each one is its own trip. The first was in the Austrian Alps, at Tanafreida above the Montafon valley in Vorarlberg. The next one is already in the works, somewhere new.

When is the next one?

We're planning it now and the dates aren't public yet. Put yourself on the waitlist and you'll be the first to hear when they are.

How big is the group?

Small. It's a handful of women, not a crowd, so there's room to actually talk and be seen. The first retreat was twelve of us.

Can you handle other dietary needs too?

Yes. Everything is gluten-free by default, and beyond that we cook around what you need, whether that's vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian or no restrictions at all. Just tell us when you sign up.

Do I need any retreat or yoga experience?

No. Most women come on their own and have never done a retreat before. Nothing is compulsory and nothing is rushed. You do as much or as little as you feel like.
